### Patterns worth copying

- **Title = category + "Singapore"** (e.g. "Leave Management System Singapore | …", "Best HRMS Software Singapore"). Same pattern that won DeepCost its AI Overview.
- **H1 repeats the exact query.** Talenox and Info-Tech H1s are the query verbatim.
- **Compliance vocabulary is the ranking signal for HR/payroll:** CPF, SDL, SHG (CDAC/SINDA/MBMF/ECF), FWL, IR8A, AIS, IRAS, MOM, Employment Act, itemised payslips, PDPA.
- **Attendance pages lead with feature nouns:** face recognition, GPS, geofencing, offline clocking, fake-GPS detection, kiosk mode, "sync with HR/payroll".
- **Leave pages list the statutory leave types:** annual, sick/medical, hospitalisation, childcare, maternity, paternity, no-pay, off-in-lieu, public holidays, leave calendar, payroll sync.
- **Grant hook:** almost every SME HR page shows "Up to 50% PSG grant" / "IMDA pre-approved". We cannot use this (DeepHR is not PSG pre-approved; see owner questions).
- **FAQ blocks** on every winner; questions are short and answerable in one sentence.
- **Listicles dominate "best X"** queries. We do not publish competitor listicles (rule); we win the non-"best" head terms with exact-match category pages and AI-quotable meta descriptions.

- Does leave data sync with payroll? Is the leave system MOM-compliant? Can employees apply online?
- Can the attendance app detect location? What radius can I set? Do employees need to register faces?
- What is the CPF contribution rate in 2026/2027? What is the OW ceiling?
- What is three-way matching? What is procure-to-pay?

### Cannibalisation plan

| Broad page (any sector) | Construction page (unchanged) | How they differ |
|---|---|---|
| hr-software-singapore | construction-hr-software | SME / any sector vs office + site teams in construction |
| payroll-software-singapore | construction-payroll-software | Statutory payroll checklist vs site attendance + work passes + levy |
| attendance-app-singapore | foreign-worker-attendance-app | Any staff away from a desk vs Work Permit holders on construction sites |
| project-cost-management-software | construction-cost-control-software | Project-based businesses vs BCA workheads, claims, retention |
| procurement-software-singapore | construction-cost-control-software | Purchase-to-pay controls vs whole-project margin |

Each solution page now lists its own group ("Also for Singapore businesses" / "Also for Singapore contractors")
and cross-links to the other group, so both sets pass relevance to each other without competing.

### Decided against

- **/solutions/project-management-software-singapore**: page 1 is task/Gantt tools. DeepTrack is not a task board, so
  the page would mismatch intent. Covered as a secondary term and an FAQ on the project cost page.
- **PSG landing page**: the top SME HR pages lean on PSG, but DeepHR is not listed as pre-approved. Revisit after the owner
  answers the PSG readiness question.
- **"Best HR software Singapore" listicle**: would need competitor names.

## 3. Facts used in guides (sources)

- Annual / sick / childcare / maternity leave: MOM eligibility-and-entitlement pages (linked in the guide).
- Public holidays 2026: MOM public holidays page.
- CPF rates from 1 Jan 2026 and rounding: CPF Board "How much CPF contributions to pay".
- OW ceiling $8,000 from 1 Jan 2026, annual ceiling $102,000: CPF Board "What is the Ordinary Wage ceiling".
- SDL 0.25%, min $2, max $11.25: GoBusiness SDL FAQ.
- Itemised payslip items (not used on pages; see owner question): MOM itemised payslips page.
