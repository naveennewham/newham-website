To price a fire protection tender in Singapore, break the scope down **system by system**: sprinklers, risers, hose reels, fire alarm, gas suppression, and pumps and tanks. Take off each system from the drawings and specification, price materials from supplier quotes and your rate library, then add labour, supports, testing and commissioning, the submission drawings and calculations, and preliminaries. Most losses on fire protection jobs come from what was left out, not from the rates. The checklist at the end covers the usual misses.

This guide is about pricing. For what the systems must achieve, see the guides on [hazard classification](/guides/sprinkler-hazard-classification-singapore), [risers](/guides/wet-and-dry-riser-requirements-singapore), [hose reels](/guides/fire-hose-reel-requirements-singapore) and [fire alarm design](/guides/fire-alarm-design-singapore). For the overall tender sequence, see the [MEP tender submission checklist](/guides/mep-tender-submission-checklist).

## Typical BOQ sections

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Preliminaries | Site staff, insurances, temporary works, attendance, programme and reporting |
| Sprinkler system | Control valve sets, sprinkler heads by type, mains and branch pipework by size and material, fittings, supports, flow switches and test valves |
| Wet and dry risers | Riser pipework, landing valves, breeching inlets, standby hoses and cabinets |
| Hose reels | Hose reels and cabinets, pipework, valves and signage |
| Fire pumps and tanks | Duty and standby pumps, jockey pumps, controllers, tanks, pump room pipework and valves |
| Fire alarm | Panels, detectors, manual call points, sounders and visual alarms, interface modules, cabling and containment |
| Gas suppression | Agent cylinders, pipework, nozzles, detection and control, room integrity testing |
| Interfaces | Connections to ACMV, lifts, access control and the building management system, listed in the cause-and-effect matrix |
| Testing and commissioning | Flushing, pressure testing, system testing, witness tests and documentation |
| Submissions and documentation | Shop drawings, hydraulic calculations, submission drawings, as-builts and O&M manuals |

## Pricing each system

1. **Read the specification by system.** Note the code edition, approved brands, pipe materials, and anything the specification adds beyond the code.
2. **Confirm what is in your scope.** Fire alarm, gas suppression, pumps or tanks are sometimes under another package. Write down every exclusion.
3. **Take off by area.** Count heads, devices and valves, and measure pipework by size and material, by building and floor, so variations can be priced later.
4. **Get supplier quotes for major equipment.** Pumps, controllers, valve sets, panels and suppression systems change with the specification; price them from current quotes.
5. **Build up the labour.** Installation, supports, testing and commissioning, at your own productivity rates.
6. **Add submissions and testing.** Drawings, calculations, PE coordination where needed, and authority inspections.
7. **Review and sign off.** Compare the cost per square metre with similar past jobs, and check every exclusion and assumption is written into the tender.

## Common misses on fire protection tenders

- Supports, hangers and seismic or vibration bracing where specified
- Pipework inside plant rooms, risers and above ceilings in back-of-house areas
- Flushing, hydrostatic testing and retesting after defects
- Temporary fire protection during construction, including rising mains that must work as the building goes up
- Submission drawings, hydraulic calculations and PE endorsement fees
- Interfaces and cause-and-effect testing with other trades
- Access equipment for high-level work
- Defects liability period attendance and spare heads
- Provisional sums the client asks you to price separately

## Common mistakes

- **Pricing from the BOQ alone.** On a remeasurable tender the BOQ quantities may be provisional; check the drawings.
- **One rate for all pipework.** Price by size, material and location; risers and plant rooms take longer.
- **No query on missing design.** If pipe sizes or pump duties are missing, raise a tender query instead of guessing.
- **Exclusions not written down.** An exclusion that isn't in the tender letter becomes your cost.
- **Late supplier quotes.** Leave time for suppliers; equipment is a large share of an FP tender.
