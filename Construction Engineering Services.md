SERVICE STREAM 1 — FIRE PROTECTION DESIGN & CALCULATIONS
Specialist FP design services for contractors and consultants. All deliverables produced to Singapore codes and formatted for authority submission.
Sprinkler System Design
System layout, head placement, pipe routing, zoning, riser diagrams. Designed to SS CP 52 and NFPA 13. All hazard classifications — ordinary, high hazard, residential, ESFR.
Hydraulic Calculations
Full hydraulic analysis per SS CP 52 / NFPA. Node-by-node calculations, demand vs supply verification, residual pressure analysis. Submission-ready reports.
Fire Pump Design & Selection
Pump sizing, duty/standby configuration, pressure-flow curve analysis, pump room layouts. Electric, diesel, and jockey pump specifications.
Fire Alarm System Design
Detection layout, zone planning, loop design, cause-and-effect matrices, panel specifications. SS CP 10 / BS 5839. Conventional and addressable systems.
Gas Suppression System Design
Clean agent (FM-200, Novec 1230) and inert gas systems. Room integrity, agent quantity calculations, pipe network sizing, nozzle placement.
Wet Riser & Dry Riser Design
Riser sizing, landing valve placement, inlet/outlet configurations, breeching inlet locations. SS CP 52 compliant.
Hose Reel System Design
Coverage planning, pipe sizing, flow and pressure analysis. Integrated with overall FP hydraulic design.
Submission Drawing Packages
Complete drawing sets — floor plans, riser diagrams, schematics, details, legends, schedules. AutoCAD/Revit. Singapore submission format. PE endorsement coordination available.

SERVICE STREAM 2 — COMMERCIAL & TENDER SUPPORT
End-to-end commercial and tender functions for M&E contractors. From first tender to final account — we handle the paperwork so your project teams can focus on delivery.
Estimation & BOQ Preparation
Material and labour quantity takeoffs, BOQ compilation, rate build-up, pricing sheets, cost summaries. Covers all M&E trades — fire protection, ACMV, ELV, plumbing, electrical.
Tender Query & Addendum Processing
RFI drafting, tender clarification tracking, addendum review and compliance marking, scope gap analysis.
Document Control & Submission Management
Drawing registers, transmittal logs, submission tracking, revision control, filing systems.
Project QS / Cost Tracking & Variations
Cost monitoring, budget vs actual tracking, variation identification and pricing, subcontractor claim verification, interim payment valuations.
Progress Claims & Payment Certification
Monthly progress claim preparation, valuation calculations, supporting documentation compilation, payment tracking.
