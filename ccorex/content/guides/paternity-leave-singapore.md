For a **Singapore citizen child born on or after 1 April 2025**, an eligible father gets **4 weeks of Government-Paid Paternity Leave (GPPL)**. Both parents can also share **shared parental leave**: **6 weeks** for children born from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026, and **10 weeks** for children born on or after **1 April 2026**. The employer pays the employee during the leave and claims it back from the Government, up to the caps.

Figures are as of October 2026, from MOM's paternity and shared parental leave pages.

## Paternity leave at a glance

| | Government-Paid Paternity Leave |
|---|---|
| Child | Singapore citizen |
| Length | 4 weeks (child born on or after 1 April 2025); 2 weeks plus 2 optional weeks before that |
| Service needed | At least 3 months of continuous service before the birth |
| Marriage | Married to the child's mother between conception and birth, or within 12 months after the birth |
| Days | Weeks × working days per week, up to 6 days a week (24 days for a 6-day worker) |
| Default timing | 4 continuous weeks within 16 weeks of the birth |
| Flexible timing (if agreed) | 4 continuous weeks, or days in any combination, within 12 months of the birth |
| Notice | 4 weeks, unless the employer agrees to less |
| Reimbursement | Up to $2,500 a week, $10,000 in total |

Employers may choose to give paternity leave to an employee who meets every condition except the 3-month service rule, and MOM says MSF may reimburse it.

## Shared parental leave

| Child's date of birth | Shared parental leave |
|---|---|
| 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 | 6 weeks |
| On or after 1 April 2026 | 10 weeks |

- **Child:** a Singapore citizen.
- **Service:** each parent needs at least 3 months of continuous service before the birth.
- **Fathers:** the same marriage condition as paternity leave.
- **Sharing:** the parents agree how the weeks are split between them.
- **Timing:** within 12 months of the birth. The default is one continuous block. Non-continuous leave needs the employer's agreement. Without agreement, it is one continuous block within 26 weeks of the birth.
- **Notice:** 4 weeks, unless the employer agrees to less.
- **Pay:** the employer pays and claims the weeks allocated to that employee, capped at $2,500 a week.

## What HR should record

- The child's date of birth (or estimated delivery date) and citizenship, which decide the entitlement.
- The employee's start date, for the 3-month rule.
- The sharing arrangement for shared parental leave, so you know how many weeks are yours to give.
- The date notice was given, and any agreement on flexible timing.
- The days taken and the pay for them, to claim reimbursement.

## Common mistakes

- **Using the old 2-week rule.** For children born on or after 1 April 2025, paternity leave is 4 weeks.
- **Assuming shared parental leave is 6 weeks.** For children born on or after 1 April 2026, it is 10 weeks.
- **Splitting the leave without agreeing it.** Without agreement, paternity leave is a continuous block within 16 weeks of the birth, and shared parental leave a continuous block within 26 weeks.
- **Expecting the Government to pay the employee directly.** The employer pays and then claims, up to the caps.
- **Not recording working days per week.** The number of leave days depends on it.
