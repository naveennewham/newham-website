Singapore contractors can get co-funding for some construction software through BCA's Built Environment Productivity Solutions Grant (BE PSG), but only for solutions on the pre-approved list and only under the terms in force when you apply. The scheme's terms are changing, so check the current position on the official pages before you buy anything. This guide explains what the grant covers, what to check, and how to decide whether a funded or unfunded tool is right for your firm.

One point up front: **CCorex is not currently listed as a PSG pre-approved solution.** We cover the grant here because contractors ask us about it, and we would rather give you accurate information than leave you to find out late.

## What the Built Environment PSG covers

According to [BCA's BE PSG page](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/grants-and-funded-programmes/built-environment-productivity-solutions-grant):

- The grant provides **up to 50% co-funding** for eligible solutions.
- For digital solutions, funding is **capped at S$50,000 per firm over five years**.
- The current tranche covers applications submitted from **1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031**.
- The grant is for **local SMEs**, and you must not have signed a contract or paid anything for the solution before you apply.
- BCA says the BE PSG is **scheduled to transition to EDGE in December 2026**, and that it will share more details closer to the date. It has not yet published how the BE PSG will work under EDGE.

When BCA announced this tranche in [January 2026](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/resources/newsroom/steady-construction-demand-in-2026-as-singapore-steps-up-support-for-built-environment-firms-through-collaboration-and-innovation/), it said the tranche would include more pre-approved digital solutions in focus areas such as **digital contract management** and coordinated regulatory approvals, extend support to advanced equipment such as robotics and automation, and raise grant caps. SMEs that used earlier PSG tranches can apply again. Which specific contract management products are supported is decided by the pre-approved list, so check it for the exact solution and package.

Do not confuse the BE PSG with Enterprise Singapore's general PSG. According to [Enterprise Singapore](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/edge-grant), its PSG, together with EDG and MRA, ceased on 29 September 2026, and businesses now apply for that support under the EDGE Grant. BCA's page states that this does not affect the BE PSG, which BCA administers and which remains open.

The December transition matters. If you are planning a software purchase for late 2026 or 2027, the terms that apply to you may not be the terms listed today. Check before you commit.

## Where to check the facts

Two official sources should be your starting point:

1. **BCA's BE PSG page:** [www1.bca.gov.sg/grants-and-funded-programmes/built-environment-productivity-solutions-grant](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/grants-and-funded-programmes/built-environment-productivity-solutions-grant). This sets out the scheme for the built environment sector and any transition updates.
2. **GoBusiness PSG directory:** [www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/](https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/productivity-solutions-grant/). BCA points applicants here to see which solutions are supported. Applications are made on the Business Grants Portal with Corppass.

A vendor telling you they are "PSG approved" is not enough. Search for the exact solution name and package on the GoBusiness list. Approval is usually for a specific package, not for everything a vendor sells.

## Checklist before you rely on a grant

- [ ] Confirm the solution and the exact package are on the GoBusiness pre-approved list
- [ ] Read the current BE PSG terms on the BCA page
- [ ] Check whether the transition to EDGE affects your timing
- [ ] Confirm your firm meets the eligibility conditions
- [ ] Check how much of your firm's funding cap is already used
- [ ] Apply before you sign a contract or pay anything
- [ ] Keep the vendor quotation that matches the approved package
- [ ] Understand what you must submit to claim after implementation

## Funded or not: how to decide

A grant reduces the cost of a solution. It does not make a poor fit into a good one. The real question is whether a tool will save your estimators, project managers and HR team enough time and money to justify its full cost.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does it fit how we tender, buy and pay? | A tool that forces a workflow you do not use will be abandoned |
| Will it still be worth it without the grant? | Grants end, caps run out and renewals are usually unfunded |
| What does it cost after the funded period? | Subscription renewals continue after any co-funding |
| How long is setup? | Loading rates, budgets and staff takes time, funded or not |
| Can we get our data out? | You may change tools later |

If a funded tool fits your workflow, the grant is a welcome bonus. If the best fit for your firm is not on the list, compare the full unfunded cost against the cost of the hours your team loses today.

## Which parts of the business to digitalise first

Contractors often try to digitalise everything at once. It is usually better to start where the money leaks fastest.

### Tendering and estimating

If your estimators price in spreadsheets with different rates in each file, errors reach submitted tenders. A shared component library and approval step fixes this. See [choosing MEP estimating software](/resources/choosing-mep-estimating-software).

### Project cost control

If purchases are approved by message and nobody sees budget against actual until month-end, overruns arrive late. Read [budget vs actual in construction](/resources/budget-vs-actual-construction).

### Attendance and payroll

If site attendance is on paper or a shared kiosk, payroll takes days to reconcile. Attendance on workers' own phones, approved by HR and flowing into payroll, removes much of that work.

### Wider digital delivery

On larger projects, the push toward integrated digital delivery and CORENET X affects how design and regulatory information flows. Commercial data from estimating and cost control sits alongside that. See our guide to [integrated digital delivery in Singapore](/resources/integrated-digital-delivery-singapore).

## Preparing your firm before any purchase

Whether or not a grant applies, the work before you buy decides whether the software is used. These steps cost nothing and make any later application or evaluation faster.

1. **Write down the problem.** For example: "tenders go out with different rates in each file" or "we only see project overruns at month-end". A clear problem statement keeps vendor demos focused.
2. **Name an owner.** One person inside the firm should own the rollout, not the vendor.
3. **Collect your data.** Rate lists, budget templates, approval rules, staff records. Most setup delays come from data nobody has gathered.
4. **Agree how you will measure success.** Pick something you can observe, such as how long a tender takes to price or how many days payroll takes to close.
5. **Plan the cut-over.** Decide when the old spreadsheets stop being used.

## Questions to ask any vendor

- Is this exact package on the GoBusiness pre-approved list today? Can you show me the listing?
- What does the price include, and what is charged separately (setup, training, extra users)?
- What happens to the price at renewal?
- How long does a typical setup take for a firm of our size, and what do you need from us?
- Can we export all our data if we leave?
- Can we trial it on one of our own projects or tenders before committing?

Ask for the answers in writing and keep them with the quotation. If you later apply for funding, the quotation and package details will need to match what is listed, and a written record avoids confusion months later when the people involved have moved on.

A vendor who answers these clearly, including saying "we are not on the list", is usually easier to work with after the sale.

## Other support to look at

Beyond the BE PSG, Singapore has other enterprise support schemes. Enterprise Singapore's [EDGE Grant](https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/edge-grant) took over from its EDG, PSG and MRA grants from 30 September 2026. Names, terms and eligibility change over time, so we do not list figures here. Start from the Enterprise Singapore and GoBusiness grants pages and BCA's grants section, and speak to the agency directly if your case is unusual.

## Common mistakes

- **Buying before applying.** Signing a contract or paying a deposit before the grant application can make the purchase ineligible.
- **Trusting "PSG approved" claims without checking.** Verify the exact package on GoBusiness yourself.
- **Assuming today's terms will apply later.** With the transition to EDGE scheduled, terms for future purchases may differ.
- **Choosing software for the grant, not the fit.** A cheaper tool that nobody uses costs more than an unfunded tool that works.
- **Forgetting renewals.** Budget for the full subscription after any funded period ends.
- **Ignoring setup effort.** The grant does not load your rates, budgets or staff records for you.
