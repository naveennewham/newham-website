BCA's **Contractors Registration System (CRS)** is Singapore's registry of construction firms. A firm registers for one or more **workheads**, the kinds of work it does, at a **grade** for each. The grade sets the **tendering limit**: the value of public sector contracts the firm can bid for. As of October 2026, registration is needed by firms that hire **construction S Pass or Work Permit holders** (since 1 June 2025), and by contractors that **tender for public sector construction work** or act as **first-level subcontractors** on it.

This guide explains the workhead groups, grades and current tendering limits, and how to prepare an application. Requirements per workhead change, so check BCA's CRS pages for the current specific registration requirements before you apply.

## Workhead groups

BCA lists about 50 workheads in five groups:

| Group | What it covers | Grades |
|---|---|---|
| CW — Construction Workheads | General building (CW01) and civil engineering (CW02) | A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2, C3 |
| CR — Construction-Related Workheads | Specialist construction trades | L6 to L1, or single grade |
| ME — Mechanical & Electrical Workheads | Building services trades | L6 to L1 |
| TR — Trade Heads | Trade work | Single grade |
| RW — Regulatory Workheads | Work regulated under other requirements | Not graded |

Examples from the ME group in BCA's directory:

| Workhead | Name |
|---|---|
| ME01 | Air-conditioning, refrigeration and ventilation works |
| ME05 | Electrical engineering |
| ME15 | Integrated building services (grades L2 to L6) |

See BCA's ME registration requirements for the full list of ME workheads and what each covers.

## Tendering limits, 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027

As of October 2026, BCA lists the following tendering limits (S$ million). They are the same as for 2025–2026.

**Construction workheads (CW01 and CW02)**

| Grade | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | C1 | C2 | C3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tendering limit | Unlimited | 105 | 50 | 16 | 5 | 1.6 | 0.8 |

**Specialist workheads (CR and ME)**

| Grade | Single grade | L6 | L5 | L4 | L3 | L2 | L1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tendering limit | Unlimited | Unlimited | 16 | 8 | 5 | 1.6 | 0.8 |

A firm can tender for public sector projects within the limit for its grade in the relevant workhead.

## What BCA assesses

Each workhead has **specific registration requirements** covering:

- **Financial:** set by grade, assessed from the firm's financial statements.
- **Track record:** projects completed in the workhead.
- **Personnel:** the qualified staff the firm employs.
- **Other requirements:** such as certifications, where the workhead calls for them.

Higher grades need more of each. BCA publishes the requirements per workhead; check them for the grade you are aiming at.

## How to apply

1. **Prepare.** Read the CRS guidelines, the terms of registration and the specific registration requirements for each workhead you want.
2. **Gather documents.** Company and financial records (for example a recent ACRA business profile and the latest accounts), personnel records and track record documents for completed projects.
3. **Submit online.** Apply through BCA's eBACS portal. Application types include new registration, additional workheads, upgrading, renewal and transfer.

## Keeping a track record ready

Upgrading a grade depends on your completed projects, so keep a record of each one as it finishes:

- Contract value, client and main contractor.
- Workhead the work falls under.
- Start and completion dates.
- Letters of award, completion certificates and final accounts.

## Common mistakes

- **Registering for the wrong workhead.** Check the workhead definitions; an M&E firm may need several ME workheads, not one.
- **Bidding above your tendering limit.** The limit for your grade in that workhead applies to each public sector tender.
- **Letting the track record go undocumented.** Upgrades need evidence of completed projects, which is hard to rebuild years later.
- **Assuming registration is only for public tenders.** Since 1 June 2025 it is also needed by firms that hire construction S Pass or Work Permit holders.
- **Using last year's requirements.** BCA revises requirements and limits; check the current pages before you apply.
