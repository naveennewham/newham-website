Childcare leave in Singapore depends on the child's citizenship and age. A working parent of a **Singapore citizen child under 7** gets **6 days of Government-Paid Childcare Leave a year**. A parent of a child under 7 who is **not** a Singapore citizen gets **2 days a year under the Employment Act**. Both need at least **3 continuous months of service**. A parent whose youngest Singapore citizen child is **7 to 12** gets **2 days of extended childcare leave**.

Figures below are as of October 2026. MOM has said the Government plans to increase childcare leave and fully reimburse employers for Singapore citizen children; dates and criteria were still to be announced, so check MOM's childcare leave pages before you change your policy.

## Childcare leave at a glance

| | Government-Paid Childcare Leave | Employment Act childcare leave | Extended childcare leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| Child | Singapore citizen, under 7 | Not a Singapore citizen, under 7 | Youngest child a Singapore citizen, aged 7 to 12 |
| Days per parent per year | 6 | 2 | 2 |
| Service needed | 3 continuous months | 3 continuous months | 3 continuous months |
| Paid by the employer | First 3 days, at the gross rate of pay | Both days | — |
| Paid by the Government | Last 3 days, up to $500 a day incl. CPF | — | Both days, up to $500 a day incl. CPF |
| Total cap per child | 42 days | 14 days | — |
| Pro-rated in the first year | Yes, minimum 2 days | No | No |

The entitlement is per parent, not per child, so a parent with two children under 7 still gets 6 days a year. Parents with children in both age groups get no more than 6 paid childcare leave days a year in total.

## Pro-rating in the first year

An employer may pro-rate the 6-day entitlement for an employee with less than a year of service. As of October 2026, MOM's table is:

| Completed months of service | Childcare leave days |
|---|---|
| Fewer than 3 | Not eligible |
| 3 to 4 | 2 |
| 5 to 6 | 3 |
| 7 to 8 | 4 |
| 9 to 10 | 5 |
| 11 or more | 6 |

The 2 days of Employment Act childcare leave and the 2 days of extended childcare leave are not pro-rated.

## The leave year

The leave year is the calendar year unless you and the employee agree on another 12-month period. Unused childcare leave cannot be carried forward.

## What HR should record

- Each child's date of birth and citizenship, which decide the type and amount of leave.
- The employee's start date, for the 3-month rule and pro-rating.
- Days taken per year, and in total per child, against the caps.
- Pay for each childcare leave day, so the Government-paid days can be claimed.

## Common mistakes

- **Giving 6 days per child.** The entitlement is per parent per year, whatever the number of children.
- **Pro-rating the Employment Act 2 days.** MOM says they cannot be pro-rated.
- **Forgetting the parent's spouse is separate.** Each parent has their own entitlement with their own employer.
- **Carrying unused days forward.** Childcare leave does not roll over.
- **Missing the age cut-off.** Childcare leave for a child under 7 changes to extended childcare leave once the youngest citizen child turns 7.
